"Let's start with the problem statement. In traditional distribution centers,
dock appointments are booked days in advance as static time slots. However, in
the real world, trucks hit highway traffic, get held up at weigh stations,
arrive hours early, or show up unbooked without a dock assignment.

When a delayed truck's actual arrival window—from its live ETA through its
30, 45, or 60-minute unloading duration—overlaps with the next scheduled truck
on the same dock, it triggers a domino effect: severe dock bay collisions,
long yard queues, expensive carrier detention fees, cold-chain spoilage risks,
and hours of manual phone calls and spreadsheet rework for warehouse planners."

SECTION 03: HOW WE SOLVE IT — 5-AGENT PIPELINE & ARCHITECTURE (00:45 - 01:20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[SCREEN CUE]: Hover across the 5 Autonomous Agent Pipeline cards (01 through 05)
              above the Gantt chart.

[SPOKEN SCRIPT]:
"Here is how we solve it. Instead of relying on static rules or manual
rescheduling, we built a decoupled four-tier architecture powered by five
specialized autonomous agents working in a continuous Sense, Reason, Act,
Decide, and Learn pipeline:

- First, Agent 01, the Truck Data Agent, connects to the Fleet TMS database to
  load incoming truck manifests, cargo requirements—like Reefer, Dry Van, or
  Flatbed—and variable appointment durations of 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
- Second, Agent 02, the Schedule Data Agent, reads the 24-hour slot occupancy
  and physical bay equipment capabilities across Dock A and Dock B from the
  Warehouse WMS database.
- Third, Agent 03, the ETA Finder Agent, ingests live GPS telematics, calculates
  exact arrival variances, and triggers our Overlapping ETA Red Indicator
  whenever two trucks' real-world arrival windows collide on the same dock.
- Fourth, Agent 04, the Slot Checking Agent, acts as our multi-constraint
  optimization engine—evaluating Pairwise Slot Swaps, Cross-Dock Swaps, Time
  Shifts, and New Slot Bookings.
- And fifth, Agent 05, the Execution and Communication Agent, keeps a human
  supervisor in the loop and atomically commits approved changes across both
  SQLite databases while dispatching instant alerts to drivers and the Master
  Scheduler."
